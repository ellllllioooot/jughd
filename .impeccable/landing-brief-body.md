# Surface brief — Landing page (Persuade)

## Scope and visitor mode
The public marketing landing page, first surface of the new world. Mode: Persuade — a logged-out visitor decides and acts. Persian, RTL; desktop and mobile.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
- **Audience:** Persian-speaking desk workers (work, study, gaming, browsing) arriving logged-out; broad consumer, not an occupation.
- **Job:** believe that Jughd catches bad posture as it happens and names the specific reason, without nagging — then start.
- **Action:** demo-first conversion — run the in-page webcam mechanism demo (permission-gated, pixels stay on-device); registration follows the first named alert.
- **Proof:** the live mechanism demo (an alert naming its reason on the visitor's own figure); real product UI as inset miniatures (labeled synthetic wherever a visitor could mistake it for real user data); a cited-science strip. No testimonials, user counts, or benchmarks — ever.
- **Constraints:** Persian-only copy, RTL, Persian numerals; zero image/video data leaves the browser; no nagging/scolding tone, no clinical/medical framing, no stock-SaaS genericism; free tier is 12 cumulative hours per calendar month; global stats render only from real aggregates.
